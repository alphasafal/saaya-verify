# SAAYA FS-01 — full-size design basis / revision A

User direction: prioritize the human-scale shelter and retain the tabletop demonstrator. Revision A is a detailed design-review and quotation package, not an issued-for-construction design.

- Site context: Vellore; exact plot, exposure, soil, drainage and utility survey are not available.
- Four-post frame, 2400 × 2000 mm post centres. Roof projection 2800 × 2400 mm. Front clear frame underside 2310 mm. Roof fall 120 mm over 2000 mm, toward the front gutter.
- Two removable roof layers, approximately 120 mm ventilating separation. Upper reflective metal skin; lower replaceable metal lining. Edge folding, overlap/seal and uplift fasteners require fabricator detailing.
- Demountable roof module on welded side/end-frame subassemblies; repeated tube sections and flat connection plates. Foot plates and anchor envelopes are geometry proposals, not engineered foundation capacity.
- Rear bench, 1400 × 450 mm, seat height 450 mm. Front route and remaining side bay stay open. These are design clearances, not an accessibility-compliance declaration.
- One guarded 200 mm 12 V fan cartridge and paired temperature/humidity stations, roof probe, fan-branch power monitor, local buttons/display/assistance and external validation ports. No claim that one fan provides adequate occupied-zone airflow.
- Outdoor fan, enclosure seals, electrical installation, structural connections and foundations are release gates. The selected Noctua fan reference establishes an interface envelope, not weather suitability.
- Optional unplumbed water experiment is isolated from the dry electronics. Core design does not use automatic evaporation or mains-powered cooling.

Deliverables: editable CadQuery source, solid STEP assembly and individual parts, STL viewing meshes, exploded placement manifest, flat plate DXF files, drawings, cut/BOM quantities, mass accounting, interference report, screening calculations and a DFM/RFQ checklist. Manufacturing tolerances are proposed acceptance targets, subject to supplier capability and first-article inspection.
