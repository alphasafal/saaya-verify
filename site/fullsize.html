2.4 × 2.0 m frame

Four post centres; 2.8 × 2.4 m nominal roof projection. Sloping double roof with a ventilating gap. Dimensions are engineering proposals.

Serviceable by design

Separate lid/carrier, replaceable screens and seat slats, guarded fan cartridge, dry electronics and isolated water experiment.

Review every assumption

STEP solids and DXFs support design review. Screening is not code approval; anchors, connections, forming details and outdoor components remain release holds.

What the model can prove.

The generated checks inspect solid validity, part quantities and geometric intersections. They do not prove strength, weather resistance, assembly fit or useful airflow. Simplified bought-out parts and unthreaded fastener envelopes are labelled in the BOM.

For the independently retained tabletop model, open the tabletop viewer. Its 40 mm fan, firmware and dimensions are not a scale model of full-size cooling performance.
