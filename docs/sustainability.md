# Sustainability and novelty hypotheses

SAAYA's candidate contribution is a repairable, climate-adaptable shelter coupled to auditable, fault-aware intervention evidence. Shade, reflective roofs, windcatchers, jaali and fans are prior art. No patentability or worldwide-first claim is established.

## Mechanism decisions

- Verandah-like deep shade: core; test sun coverage through the day, not only noon.
- Double roof: core experiment; log both surface temperature and air conditions. Reflective finish remains serviceable and replaceable.
- Jaali: interchangeable coupon; characterize pressure loss/air-speed effects before selecting porosity. A venturi-like local speed increase does not establish bulk-air cooling.
- Windcatcher/high exhaust: research extension; performance depends on ambient wind, geometry and nearby obstructions. No unsupported miniature-to-full-scale scaling.
- Thermal mass/night flushing: exclude from the lightweight first model; usefulness depends on diurnal swing, night conditions, mass and timing.
- Terracotta evaporation: defer as separate module; verify climate, water use, maintenance and air quality.

## Accounting boundary

Report four quantities separately: bill-of-material mass; measured electricity; measured non-potable cooling-water use; maintenance/replacement. Drinking-water availability is a worker amenity, not cooling-water savings. Solar production is not automatically a net lifecycle benefit: include panel/battery manufacture, replacement and end-of-life if added.

`cad/exports/mesh-report.json` reports solid volumes. Multiplying by a material density yields an upper-bound solid mass, not slicer consumption or embodied carbon. For purchasing, slice the actual chosen print settings including supports and scrap, then measure material used. Roof panel area at v2 is0.0832m² per layer; two layers0.1664m². Full-size estimates use a separate roughly2×2m footprint and about5m² per roof layer; this is a conceptual allowance, not an engineered bill.

Fasteners and removable panels support repair; standard low-voltage parts allow replacement. Track cleaning time, dirty-roof performance, failed fans and sensor drift. Avoid adhesives where mechanical fastening works. Do not assert bamboo/mycelium/terracotta is sustainable merely because it is natural; include durability, coating and transport.

## Cost decision

The source-backed and allowance-based tabletop BOM is not a production unit cost. The full-size estimate includes frame, roofs, bench/floor, electronics, electrical installation, foundations, labour, transport and contingency, but remains unquoted. It spans the listed AC equipment price. A local installed AC quote and a shelter fabrication quote are both required to decide whether the target is met. No claim of equivalent thermal service is appropriate for an open shelter versus an enclosed-room AC.
